# Meridian Mutual Claims Handbook (extract)

Internal guidance for claims handlers. Version 2026.3. Contains no customer data.

## Escape of water

Escape of water claims cover sudden leaks from fixed water installations and domestic appliances, including burst pipes and failed washing-machine hoses. The standard excess for an escape of water claim is £350. Drying costs are covered once a Meridian-approved contractor confirms the source of the leak has been repaired. Gradual seepage over more than 30 days is not covered.

## Subsidence

Subsidence claims carry a £1,000 excess. A structural engineer's report is required before any reserve above £10,000 is set. Monitoring normally runs for 12 months before repairs are authorised.

## Storm damage

Storm damage is covered where wind speeds of 48 knots or more are recorded at the nearest weather station, or where hail or snow causes direct physical damage. The standard excess for storm damage is £250. Fences, gates and hedges are excluded.

## Theft

Theft claims over £2,000 require a police crime reference number before settlement. The standard excess for theft is £200. Theft by a person lawfully in the home is covered only under the accidental damage extension.

## Accidental damage

Accidental damage is an optional extension. The excess is £150. Wear and tear, pet damage and damage caused while the home is let are excluded.

## Fraud indicators

Fraud indicators that require referral to the Special Investigations Unit include a policy incepted within 30 days of the loss, three or more claims at the same address in 24 months, and receipts that cannot be verified with the retailer. A handler must never tell the customer that a referral has been made.

## Service standards

Every new claim is acknowledged within one working day. A handler contacts the customer within two working days. Claims under £1,500 with clear liability may be settled by the handler without a loss adjuster.
